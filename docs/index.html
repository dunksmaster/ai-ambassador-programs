The Ambassador Ledger

What each program is about, and what ambassadors actually do — not a link dump.

Kept by Dorian Kane (dunksmaster), also Kane. A working index for someone who is not a student applicant and does not want to guess from a landing-page slogan.

How to read this

Every entry names the product, then the work: events, content, feedback, campus clubs, paid writing. Status pills are current as of 31 August 2026. Gated marks student-only or audience-threshold programs. Student-only rows are hidden unless you turn them on — this ledger defaults to seats Kane can actually take.

What this is not

It is not an application service and not a perk catalog invented after the fact. Closed and paused programs stay on the page so the record does not pretend they vanished. When a shorter card could not be independently dated, the stamp is See site — open the official URL before you spend an evening on a form.
